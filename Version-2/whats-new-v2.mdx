## 1. New modules

Kontratar reads each announcement and pulls out the eligibility rules, the funding amount, and the list of application components, so you can rule a grant in or out without reading the full notice. ## 2. New AI capabilities

Three AI features are now available in both Awards and Forecasts. They work the same way in each module.

### AI Chat

Ask a question in ordinary English and get the answer back as a table of records.

You do not build filters or write a query. Type something like "Show me all awards for Adept Engineering Solutions" and Kontratar reads the database and returns the matching rows. If you want the AI to take a document into account, attach it to the question. A capability statement or a list of target agencies both work.

Every answer has three parts:

1. **Reasoned.** A collapsed section. Open it to see how the AI understood your question before it searched. If a result looks wrong, check here first.
2. **The results table.** Scroll sideways for columns that run past the edge. Copy and download controls sit above the top right corner of the table.
3. **The summary.** A short paragraph saying what the records cover, including the exact date range the AI used. If you asked for "the last two years" and it picked the wrong window, this is where you will notice.

### Projects

A project is a research workspace with its own instructions and files.

You write a set of standing instructions once, and every chat you start inside that project follows them. Say your instruction is "Only focus on Department of Agriculture awards." You then ask "What are the awards available today?" and you get Department of Agriculture awards back. You did not repeat the constraint in your question. The project applied it for you.

You can also upload reference files to a project. Kontratar accepts PDF, CSV, Excel, and Word documents. Instructions and files work together, so a project scoped to one agency and loaded with your capability statement will filter to that agency and weigh the results against what you actually do.

### Scheduled Research

A scheduled research item is a question that runs on its own at a set interval.

Pick daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly. Kontratar runs the query for you and files each result under the date it ran. Because the runs are stored separately rather than overwriting each other, you can open one research item and read last month's answer next to this month's. A normal search only shows you the current state, so this is the only way to see what changed.

You can email the results to yourself or export them as Word, Excel, or CSV.

## 3. Pipeline award matching

Your pipeline records are now checked against the awards in Kontratar.

When a pursuit sits in your pipeline, Kontratar compares it against the award database. If it finds a matching award, it checks the recipient against your organization's SAM UEI ID. Pursuits with no matching award stay on the pipeline as live work. Pursuits you won feed the Won and Win Rate figures on the dashboard.

This is why the dashboard needs your UEI. Kontratar can see that a contract was awarded, but it has no way of knowing whether your organization was the winner unless it has your identifier to check against.

<Warning>
  **Pipeline metrics need a SAM UEI ID.** If your tenant does not have one set, the dashboard shows `Charts unavailable, SAM UEI ID is required to calculate pipeline metrics`, and the Won and Win Rate cards show `N/A`. Total Records and Active still work, because those count your own records and do not depend on matching.
</Warning>

## 4. What stays the same

Opportunities, Proposals, Simulations, Documents, Categories, and Settings work the way they always did. There is no migration step, and the records you created in the previous version are still there.
